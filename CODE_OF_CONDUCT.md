# Code of conduct

udpstp follows the [Rust Code of Conduct](https://www.rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct). In short: be kind and courteous, assume good faith, respect different opinions, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

It applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the details at [pederbe.dev](https://pederbe.dev). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, and may remove their comments, commits and issues.
